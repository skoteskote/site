*You cannot hear each other, you cannot see each other, you can only feel each other's warmth.*
{: .lede}

Reach combines hand clay sculpting, traditional bronze casting, electronics, and spatial computing. The project consists of three bronzes connected through a virtual augmented reality layer. When you touch one sculpture, 300 temperature sensors inside pick up your touch and transmit it to the others, through AR, vibrations and sound.

With your hand across its surface, you communicate in silence, in secret. A virtual layer allows you to see these silent words of warmth, flowing from the hands of those who feel its distant parts. Reach merges ancient craft with cutting-edge technology, to form a collective experience based on our most intimate byproduct: body heat.

> Bronze sculptures that help you feel another's presence through the warmth of your hand, visualised through AR but felt through your heart. If Rothko was alive today, this is how he might communicate his feelings.
>
> Mark Hayes-Westall, FAD Magazine
